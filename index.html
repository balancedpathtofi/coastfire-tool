What is Coast FIRE?

Retirement handled.
Career optional.

Coast FIRE isn't about quitting your job. It's about reaching the point where compound interest will grow your existing investments into a full retirement — without another dollar of contributions.

There's a specific number — your Coast number — where the math is done. Everything you earn after that is yours to use however you want. A lower-stress role. A sabbatical. A career bet you couldn't afford to take before.

"Coast FIRE allows you to fund your future self early so your current self can afford to breathe."— For high-achieving professionals who've done everything right and still feel trapped.

Most high earners are optimizing for a finish line they've never clearly defined. Coast FIRE gives you a number. A real, specific number. And for people who've been aggressively saving since their late 20s, that number is often a lot closer than it feels.
